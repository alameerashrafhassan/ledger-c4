cashback System

System-owned account type for cashback bookkeeping. Defined in Account::ACCOUNT_TYPES and flagged as a system account.

No flow currently writes to this account via Ledger::CreateTransaction. Cashbacks::BulkCreator persists Cashback rows but does not route through the ledger service. Documenting only what's wired today.
