Chargeback settlement

An ops user marks a pending chargeback as approved. The disputed amount is credited to the cardholder's cash account from the system suspense account. Implementation: Chargebacks::Settle.

Actors

  • Initiator: Operations / Treasury user
  • Affected: Cardholder (cash account credited), system suspense (debited)
  • System: Chargebacks::Settle service

Pre-conditions

  • chargeback.status == pending (enforced under with_lock)
  • Params include status: 'approved'
  • A wallet exists matching chargeback.decorated_fast_message.account_id
  • System suspense account exists (Account.suspense.first!)

Post-conditions

  • chargeback.status == completed on success, failed otherwise
  • One LedgerTransaction with referrer = chargeback
  • SMS notification job enqueued on success

Reconciliation impact

  • Suspense should drain to zero in steady state. Persistent positive balance signals stuck or lost approvals.
  • Each settled chargeback creates exactly one LedgerTransaction. Counting these against externally-reported chargeback counts is the recon check.
