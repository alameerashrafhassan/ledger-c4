Purpose

Decomposes the Sidekiq Workers container into job families. Useful when adding a new background job: tells you where it belongs and which downstream resources it can touch.

Decisions & Notes

  • One ApplicationJob. All workers inherit common retry / error policy from a single base; new workers must extend it, not Sidekiq::Worker directly, to inherit tenancy scoping.
  • Cron is opinionated. Schedules live in config/sidekiq.yml via sidekiq-scheduler — not in cron.d or k8s CronJobs — to keep them code-reviewed.
  • Heavy export work is async. Controllers never block on report generation; they enqueue an Email Exports Job.

Assumptions

  • Job groupings reflect app/jobs/ folder structure; minor jobs are folded into the closest family.
  • Single Sidekiq process per environment is assumed; if queues are split into dedicated workers (e.g. a separate "exports" worker), that becomes a new container in Level 2.
