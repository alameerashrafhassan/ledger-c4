cash Beneficiary-owned

The wallet account of an individual beneficiary (employee / cardholder). Holds the spendable balance after disbursements, chargebacks and any other ledger credit.

Definition

Account.typecash
Accountablepolymorphic — typically Beneficiary via Wallet
System account?No (must have an accountable)
Local?Yes (cash and global_cash are flagged local?)
Tracked balancesposted_balance_cents, available_balance_cents

Flows that touch this account

FlowDirectionDriver
Salary disbursementCreditDisbursements::SubProcessor via Ledger::CreateTransaction(disbursement → cash)
Chargeback settlementCreditChargebacks::Settle via Ledger::CreateTransaction(suspense → cash)
ReversalEitherLedger::RevertTransaction when the original touched a cash account

Notes

  • Card spend and CMS-side debits are not modelled as ledger debits in the repo today; they're handled CMS-side via balance updates.
  • The wallet is associated to a card via Wallet has_one :card; the virtual_account_id is the link to the CMS-side account.
