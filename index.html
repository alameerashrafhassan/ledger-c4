PayEngine Ledger — Money Movement

PayEngine 2.0 is a Rails-based double-entry ledger for the dopay/Liberty card programme. Every money movement is recorded as a LedgerTransaction with two LedgerEntry rows (one debit, one credit) created atomically via Ledger::CreateTransaction. This site documents the flows that actually exist in the codebase — nothing is fabricated.

Ledger model in one paragraph

An Account belongs to a Company, a Beneficiary, or is a system account (global_cash, cashback, suspense). Each account tracks posted_balance_cents and available_balance_cents. A LedgerTransaction groups two LedgerEntry rows (debit + credit, equal amount) and may reference a polymorphic referrer (Disbursement, Chargeback, etc.). Reversals are first-class — LedgerTransaction#reversal_transaction links the original to its inverse. All currency is EGP today; money-rails is in place if that changes.

Business flows

Salary disbursement
Disbursements::Processor + SubProcessor · referrer: Disbursement / EmployeeDisbursement
Company corporate → disbursement → many employee cash accounts. Driven per cycle. EWA settlement folds in at the start.
Chargeback settlement
Chargebacks::Settle · referrer: Chargeback
Approved chargeback credits cardholder from the system suspense account.
Ledger reversal
Ledger::RevertTransaction
Any posted transaction can be reversed; original and reversal stay linked via reversal_transaction_id.

What is intentionally NOT here

  • Wallet-to-wallet transfers — The Transfer model exists but no service in the repo calls Ledger::CreateTransaction on it; it operates as a record-keeping layer over CMS-side movements.
  • Cashback flow — Cashbacks::BulkCreator only persists Cashback rows; no ledger writes were found.
  • Earned-wage as a separate flow — EWA is folded into the salary disbursement via EarnedWagesResolver; it does not have its own ledger pathway.
  • FX, multi-currency — All amounts are EGP today; money-rails is wired but no FX conversion path is documented.
  • End-of-day reconciliation flow — No batch reconciliation service was located; reconciliation impact is noted on each flow page instead.
