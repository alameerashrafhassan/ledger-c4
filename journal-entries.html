Journal entry reference

Every Dr/Cr template that exists in the codebase today, anchored so flow pages can deep-link to the relevant pattern. Each pattern is keyed by the Ledger::CreateTransaction call-site that produces it.

Pattern: reversal

A new LedgerTransaction with the original's debit and credit accounts swapped.

No referrer is passed into the inner CreateTransaction; the linkage is via original.reversal_transaction_id.

Validation contract for every pattern

  • Every LedgerTransaction has exactly two LedgerEntry rows (one debit, one credit) of equal amount.
  • Both entries share the same ledger_transaction_id and are created inside a single ApplicationRecord.transaction.
  • Ledger::Transactions::Validator runs before posting; failures flip the transaction to status: :failure and persist the errors on the row (transaction_errors column).
  • LedgerTransaction.trx_uuid is unique — duplicate posts are blocked at the DB level.

Patterns explicitly NOT in the ledger

  • Card spend / point-of-sale debits — handled CMS-side, not modelled here.
  • Cashback bookings — Cashbacks::BulkCreator persists the row but does not write to the ledger.
  • Wallet-to-wallet Transfer records — exist as DB rows; no Ledger::CreateTransaction caller in the repo.
  • Corporate top-up from a bank rail — not present as a documented ledger pattern.
