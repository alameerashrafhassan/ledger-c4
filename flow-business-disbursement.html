Salary disbursement

A company funds a payroll cycle: money is loaded from the company's corporate account into a per-cycle disbursement holding account, then paid out one employee at a time into each employee's cash account. Implementation: Disbursements::Processor + Disbursements::SubProcessor.

Actors

  • Initiator: Corporate Admin (creates and approves the disbursement)
  • Affected: Company (corporate + disbursement accounts), Employees (cash accounts)
  • System: Disbursements::Processor running in Sidekiq

Pre-conditions

  • Disbursement.status == approved (Processor uses Disbursement.approved.find)
  • company.corporate_account and company.disbursement_account both present
  • Each EmployeeDisbursement has a non-nil employee.account
  • Disbursement is not already in_progress (enforced via with_lock)
  • EWA settlement (EarnedWagesResolver) returns true

Post-conditions

  • Disbursement.status == completed if every EmployeeDisbursement reached completed; else failed
  • Disbursement holding account back to zero (assuming all employees paid)
  • DisburserMailer + Notifications::Disbursements::Success fired
  • Successful disbursements publish to Segment via Analytics::SegmentPublisherJob
  • If failed, UnlinkEarnedWagesJob is enqueued

Reconciliation impact

  • The disbursement holding account should net to zero by end of cycle. A non-zero balance after a failed cycle is the recon signal.
  • Sum of credits to employee.cash for the cycle should equal the load amount in step 1.
  • Failed cycles leave partial credits posted; ops resolve via reversal on each successful step that should not have shipped.
