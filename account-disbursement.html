disbursement Company-owned (holding)

Per-cycle holding account: corporate funds flow in once via the load step, then flow out one transaction per employee. In a successful cycle, this account nets to zero by the end of processing.

Reconciliation invariant

  • For a fully successful cycle: balance change = 0.
  • For a failed cycle: residual balance = sum(net_amount of failed employees). Persistent residuals are the recon signal.
